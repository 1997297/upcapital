============================================================
1. WHAT UPCAPITAL SHOULD BECOME
============================================================

UPCAPITAL should feel like a combination of:

premium fintech website + investment client portal + secure financial dashboard + transparent trading/performance platform.

There are essentially three products inside one system:

1. Public Website
   - Sell the product
   - Explain how it works
   - Build trust

2. Client Cabinet
   - Allow authenticated users to manage their account and investments

3. Admin Platform
   - Allow UPCAPITAL staff to operate the business

The project should NOT be built as one giant HTML page.

Important:
Claims such as “98.9% accuracy” and “80% ROI in 30 Days” should not be hard-coded into a production investment platform unless independently substantiated and legally appropriate. Performance numbers, plans and financial disclosures should be administrator-controlled data, not fake/static marketing numbers.

UPCAPITAL — MASTER PRODUCT & DEVELOPMENT SPECIFICATION

You are the lead product engineer, frontend engineer, backend architect, UI/UX designer and security-conscious technical advisor responsible for developing UPCAPITAL.

Your responsibility is not merely to produce visually attractive code. Build UPCAPITAL as a coherent, scalable, secure, maintainable fintech-style investment platform with a premium institutional appearance.

Before implementing anything, study this specification carefully and preserve its architecture, terminology, visual system and interaction patterns throughout the project.

PRODUCT

Product name:
UPCAPITAL

Core positioning:
"Put your capital to work intelligently."

UPCAPITAL is a technology-driven digital asset/investment platform that gives users access to portfolio tracking, strategy monitoring, performance reporting, deposits, withdrawals, account security and investment management.

The application consists of three major systems:

1. Public marketing website
2. Authenticated client cabinet
3. Protected administrative dashboard

The application must never present fabricated investment activity, fake testimonials, fake withdrawals, fabricated regulatory credentials, guaranteed profits or invented performance statistics.

Any performance values must either be clearly identified demo data during development or originate from actual configured data once the system is operational.

Avoid misleading language such as:
"Guaranteed returns"
"Risk-free"
"Guaranteed income"
"98.9% win rate"

unless the platform owner later provides independently verifiable evidence and confirms that the claim may legally be published.

- PostgreSQL

- Lucide React
